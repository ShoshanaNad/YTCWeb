Privacy Policy

Introduction

Yeshivas Toras Chaim (“we,” “our,” or “the Yeshiva”) is committed to protecting the privacy of our students, parents, staff, donors, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Address
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Emergency contact information
  • Student registration information
  • Donation or payment information (processed securely through third‑party providers)

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device identifiers
  • Pages visited
  • Basic analytics data

Information Provided Through Forms

If you complete forms (e.g., registration, event sign‑ups, SMS opt‑in), we collect the information you submit voluntarily.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for legitimate organizational purposes, including:

  • Communicating with parents, students, and community members
  • Processing registrations, tuition payments, and donations
  • Sending updates, announcements, and optional SMS notifications
  • Maintaining internal records
  • Improving our website and services
We do not sell personal information.

Text Messaging (SMS) Communications

If you choose to opt in to SMS communications, we may send informational text messages related to school updates, scheduling, events, or urgent notifications.

  • Message frequency varies
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • You may opt out at any time by replying STOP
SMS participation is optional and not required for registration.

Sharing Information

We do not share personal information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information only with trusted service providers who assist with:

  • Payment processing
  • Website hosting
  • Communication systems
  • Recordkeeping
These providers are required to maintain confidentiality and use information only for the services they provide.

Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality and analyze usage. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not function properly.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13 without parental consent.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Request access to the information we hold about you
  • Request corrections
  • Opt out of non‑essential communications (including SMS)
  • Request deletion of certain information, subject to legal or operational requirements
