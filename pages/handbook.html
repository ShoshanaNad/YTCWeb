Yeshivas Toras Chaim Policies

Attendance

  • Every Talmid must attend all sedarim and tefilos unless permission was given from the Hanhalah.
  • A Talmid may not leave Yeshiva grounds unless permission was given from the Hanhalah.
  • Routine medical and dental appointments should be scheduled for Friday afternoon and off-days.
  • A Talmid may not miss Yeshiva to travel out of town without permission.

Electronics

  • No electronic devices such as iPods, smart watches, or radios may be brought to Yeshiva.
  • No cell phones are permitted in Yeshiva.
  • Recorders and MP3 players without screens are permitted but not encouraged.

Food

  • No food may be ordered for delivery to Yeshiva.
  • Parents are discouraged from delivering meals to their children.
  • No homemade food should be brought for the tzibbur.

Smoking

  • The Yeshiva has a strict no-smoking policy, including vaping and electronic cigarettes.

Alcohol

  • Possession or consumption of alcohol is strictly forbidden at all times.

Shabbos

Shabbos in Yeshiva is an integral part of the overall Yeshiva experience...

  • Talmidim must bring their own linens.
  • Tznius guidelines apply even within dorm rooms.
  • E-bikes, scooters, and similar devices are not allowed.

General Information

  • After daylight savings time, there will be Seder Motzei Shabbos.
  • Friday schedule varies by season.
