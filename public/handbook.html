Yeshivas Toras Chaim Policies

The following Yeshiva policies are essential to our mission of creating true Bnei Torah. They are designed to make every Talmid's experience pleasant and productive, and to create an environment in which each Bochur can unleash his unlimited potential. We can only accomplish this with your support and cooperation.

ATTENDANCE • Every Talmid must attend all sedarim and tefilos, unless permission was given from the Hanhalah. • A Talmid may not leave Yeshiva grounds unless permission was given from the Hanhalah. • If possible, all routine medical and dental appointments should be scheduled for Friday afternoon and off-days. • A Talmid may not miss Yeshiva to travel out of town (including Simchas, family trips, etc.). For special occasions, permission must be obtained from the Hanhalah before plans are made. ELECTRONICS • To ensure the proper ruach in Yeshiva, all electronic devices, such as ipods, smart watches, radios, etc. may not be brought to Yeshiva. Recorders and Mp3 players without screens are permitted, but not encouraged. • No cell phones are permitted in Yeshiva. Landline phones are available for the Talmidim to make and receive phone calls. Based on our experience, it is strongly discouraged for a Mesivta bachur to have a personal cell phone. If you believe your son presents an exceptional circumstance, please consult with the Hanhalas HaYeshiva. Under no circumstances can a Bachur have texting. FOOD • No food may be ordered to be delivered to Yeshiva. In addition, parents are discouraged from delivering meals to their children. • No homemade food should be brought for the Tzibbur [e.g. for a Siyum]. SMOKING • The Yeshiva has a strict no smoking policy. This includes vaping and electronic cigarettes. As with all other Yeshiva policies, this applies equally while in the van and the Shabbos dorm. ALCOHOL • Possession and/or consumption of alcohol of any type is strictly forbidden in Yeshiva at all times. SHABBOS Shabbos in Yeshiva is an integral part of the overall Yeshiva experience. The Sedorim, Seudos, Tefillos, and the chance to spend time with Rebbeim in a different setting are wonderful growth opportunities for the Bochurim. In addition, Shabbos in Yeshiva allows for the cumulative effect of to Retzifus to the Bochurim's Shteiging. To facilitate this, Yeshiva will be providing a dormitory for Shabbos. • Talmidim are expected to bring their own linens, including blankets, towels and pillows. • Within the dormitory building, even within their own rooms, Talmidim are expected to be dressed modestly in accordance with the laws of Tznius. Pajama pants should be full length, and sleeveless undershirts are not allowed. Talmidim are expected to wear robes over their pajamas when leaving the privacy of their rooms. E bikes, electric scooters, segways etc. are not allowed. • Based on our experience, it is strongly discouraged for a Mesivta bachur to obtain a driving permit or license. If you believe your son presents an exceptional circumstance, please consult with the Hanhalas HaYeshiva prior to pursuing this. All items brought into Yeshiva or the dormitory are done with the following understanding: • Any item deemed objectionable at the Yeshiva's discretion will be confiscated and may not be returned. • Any item left after each Zman will be discarded. GENERAL INFORMATION • After the daylight savings time, there will be Seder Motzei Shabbos in Yeshiva. The Seder is mandatory. Fathers are encouraged to come. • Friday schedule: From Pesach until Sukkos, Seder ends at 1:00 pm From Sukkos until Pesach, Seder ends at 12:30 pm • Ta'aneisim: On a Ta'anis, Seder ends after Mincha • Bochurim phone numbers: 216-331-2454 | 216-331-4588 SHABBOS • There are many variables and factors that decide which Shabbosim should be an In-Shabbos. Parents will be informed when there is an In-Shabbos. • Talmidim arrive Erev Shabbos already prepared and dressed for Shabbos, and depart Motzei Shabbos. • The Shabbos dormitory is located on the same block as the Yeshiva. The address is 4598 College Road, South Euclid, OH. • Approximately every four weeks there will be a short Off-Shabbos from Thursday afternoon until Sunday morning.
